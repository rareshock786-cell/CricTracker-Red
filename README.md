🏏 CricTracker — Your Personal Cricket Journal & Progress Tracker

Want to keep track of your cricket journey, training, matches, and progress all in one place?

CricTracker is a personal cricket management app designed to help you record, organize, and understand your cricket journey.

🔥 What can you do with CricTracker?

📖 Cricket Journal
Write and save detailed training sessions, match experiences, thoughts, improvements, and important moments from your cricket journey.

📊 Track Your Progress
Keep an organized record of your cricket development and see how you're progressing over time.

🎯 Set Goals
Create personal cricket goals and keep track of what you're working towards.

📅 Calendar & History
Keep your cricket activities organized and look back at previous sessions and entries.

📈 Progress Analytics
Review your activity and progress through the app's analytics features.

🔐 Private & Personal
Your CricTracker data is stored locally on your own device/browser, so different users have their own separate data.

🌐 Works on the Web
No complicated installation is required. Just open the link and start using it.

📱 Install it like an App
On supported browsers such as Chrome or Edge, CricTracker can be installed as a standalone app on your device.

💾 Offline-Friendly
Once the app has been loaded and cached, many of its core functions can continue working without an internet connection.

🎨 Clean Cricket-Focused Interface
A modern blue-themed interface designed specifically around keeping your cricket journey organized.
